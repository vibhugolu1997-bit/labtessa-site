8 real problems in testing labs, and how a LIMS fixes them

Vaibhav Singh, founder · 10 November 2026 · 6 min read

Most testing labs don't lose time on the test itself. They lose it before and after: typing in requests, choosing the right tests, checking calculations, fixing reports and answering "is this certificate real?" emails. These are eight problems we hear about again and again, and how LabTessa deals with each one.

1Every request is typed in by hand

A client sends an email, a PDF or a WhatsApp photo listing five samples and the tests they want. Someone in the lab reads it and types it all into a register or spreadsheet. It's slow, and a single slip, such as a wrong batch number or a missed test, follows the sample all the way to the report.

TodayRe-typing every request, then checking it again.
With LabTessaPaste or upload the request. LabTessa drafts the samples and tests and shows the exact words each field came from. Nothing is saved until your staff check it and confirm.

AI intake is optional. It stays off until a lab admin turns it on, and manual entry always works.

2The wrong test, or a test you don't offer

Clients write "check for heavy metals" or "full microbiology". Staff have to map that to your actual methods, matrices and prices, and mistakes are easy to make.

TodayEach person matches the client's words to tests in their own way.
With LabTessaTests come only from your own catalogue. If a request asks for something you don't offer, the draft says so instead of guessing.

3Calculation slips in spreadsheets

Derived results such as Total THC (delta-9 THC + 0.877 × THCA), moisture-corrected values or averages are often worked out in a spreadsheet and copied across. One broken formula or copy-paste error can go unnoticed for months.

TodayFormulas live in personal spreadsheets, and results are copied by hand.
With LabTessaCalculated tests use the formula and factors your lab enters once. Each result is worked out the same way every time, from the values recorded in the system.

4Results checked against the wrong limit

Is 0.4 mg/kg a pass or a fail? It depends on the product, the regulation and the edition. When limits sit in someone's head or an old printout, mistakes reach clients.

TodayLimits are looked up by hand, and the source isn't recorded.
With LabTessaEach limit is stored with its source, so the report shows what the result was checked against. Label claims can be compared with the measured result within the tolerance you set.

5The accreditation mark on tests outside your scope

Putting an NABL or ISO/IEC 17025 mark on a test that isn't in your accredited scope is a common audit finding, and it's easy to do when one report template is used for everything.

TodayOne template, with the mark on every report.
With LabTessaScope is recorded per test and matrix. The report marks only the tests that are inside your scope.

6One person does everything

In a busy lab, the analyst who entered the result often releases the report too. ISO/IEC 17025 expects results to be reviewed, and auditors look for evidence that they were.

TodayA signature on paper, if there's time.
With LabTessaSecond-person approval is built in. The person who entered a result can't also approve it, and every step is recorded with who did it and when.

7Reports quietly edited after release

A client asks for a small change, a file is edited and re-sent, and now two versions of the same report exist. Months later, nobody can say which one is right.

TodayEdit the Word file and send it again.
With LabTessaResults are write-once. A change creates a revised report that says it replaces the earlier one, and the full audit trail shows what changed and why.

8"Is this certificate real?"

Brands forward test reports and COAs to retailers and buyers, and fake or altered certificates are a real problem. Labs end up answering verification emails one by one.

TodayBuyers email or phone the lab to check.
With LabTessaEvery report and COA has a QR code. Scanning it shows whether the report is genuine and whether it has been revised, without showing the client's name or results, so it's safe to print on packaging.
Behind all eight: sample receipt with chain of custody and field sampling, nightly encrypted backups, and your data hosted in India. LabTessa is built around ISO/IEC 17025 practice. Your accreditation body assesses your lab, not the software, but these features are designed to make that assessment simpler.

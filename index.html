What it does

AI-assisted request intake

Paste an email or upload a PDF. LabTessa drafts the request (client, matrix, samples, tests) and shows the exact text each field came from. Nothing is saved until your staff confirm it.

Your catalogue, your scope

Tests can only come from your own catalogue. A test outside your accreditation scope for that matrix is flagged; it is never added silently.

Results with four-eyes approval

Results are checked against the limits you enter, with their source. The person who approves a result must be different from the one who entered it.

Honest test reports

PDF reports show the accreditation mark only for tests within scope. Corrections are issued as revised reports; the original is never changed.

Tamper-evident audit trail

Every change is recorded in a hash-chained audit trail. Results and reports are write-once.

Built for data protection

Each lab's data is isolated at the database level. Admins sign in with two-step verification. Contact details are masked before any text is sent to the AI.
